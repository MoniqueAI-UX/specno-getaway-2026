🌵 Explore Karoo1

Karoo1 Hotel & Village

Wake up to mountain views, fresh Karoo air, and wide-open spaces. Our group has access to dedicated spaces where we can meet, relax, and spend time together — a place built for switching off.

🐑 Karoo1 is a working farm, home to friendly sheep and the famous sheepdog, Benji. Don't be surprised if you make a few four-legged friends.
🍽️ Great foodHugo's Restaurant, Benji's Bar & Brasserie, and a special Boma evening.
🤝 Exclusive accessDedicated spaces for our group to meet and relax together.
📶 Wi-FiAvailable throughout Karoo1, including the accommodation, restaurant and tents.
☕ Arriving early?You're welcome to grab a coffee and work from the restaurant/coffee shop before check-in.

🎒 Pack Smart

Your mission: pack light, pack right

You don't need your whole wardrobe — just the essentials for a weekend of adventure, downtime, good food and unpredictable Karoo weather. Tap an item to check it off (nothing's saved on refresh).

👕 The Everyday Stuff
  • Comfortable clothes — t-shirts, jeans, shorts
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Warm hoodie or jacket
  • Beanie
  • Sun hat
  • Rain jacket
🏊 Pool & Adventure Ready
  • Swimming costume
  • Extra swimming towel
  • Clothes you don't mind getting dusty or dirty
🎨 Team Colour
  • Something in your team colour for Thursday — 🔵 Blue, ⚪ White or 🟢 Green

A shirt, cap, bandana or accessory is fine — just make it visible for the Potjie Challenge.

🏃 Joining the Morning Crew?
  • Running/hiking shoes
  • Activewear
  • Water bottle
🧴 The Personal Stuff
  • Toiletries, sunscreen, insect repellent
  • Medication, if needed
  • Contacts/glasses, makeup, if you'd like
📱 Don't Forget
  • Phone charger & power bank
  • Sunglasses 😎
  • A small day bag/backpack
  • Your phone — you may need it for more than photos 👀

☕ For the Snackers & Coffee People

The units are self-catering, so bring along any personal favourites: your coffee or tea, late-night snacks, drinks, road-trip snacks. Meals are covered — this is purely for "I need coffee before speaking to humans" situations.

Karoo rule: be prepared for sunshine during the day and cooler evenings.

Most importantly: bring your team spirit, competitive side, and sense of adventure. We've got the rest covered. Mission: Karoo awaits. 🚀
